# Quality agreement — Wisla Metalworks Sp. z o.o. (PL)

Commodity scope: 10,70. Measured on-time delivery over the last twelve months
is 79.2 %, quality score 5 of 5.

## Obligations

The supplier shall notify Kestrel within two working days of any event likely to delay a
confirmed delivery date by more than five working days. First-article inspection reports are
required for every new revision.

## Escalation

This supplier is currently on the watch list. On-time delivery has been below the 85 % threshold for two consecutive quarters. Purchasing is to hold a monthly review and a second source is to be qualified for all long-lead items in scope.

## Capacity

Foundry and machining capacity is confirmed quarterly. Peak constraint is typically the
late-summer shutdown period in July and August, when European foundries run reduced shifts.
